How it works

Three taps from cover to vault.

No lock screen to give the game away. The disguise is the door.

It looks like a calculator

On your home screen and in anyone's hands, Wisp is an ordinary, fully working calculator (or gallery, or reminders — your choice).

Your secret gesture opens it

A private pattern or code you set during onboarding quietly unlocks the real, encrypted vault. Face ID makes it instant.

A wrong code opens a decoy

Anything else routes silently to a believable decoy gallery. The same wrong code always shows the same harmless photos — so it looks completely real.

Privacy that hides itself

Your vault never leaves your phone.

Your encrypted photos, contacts and browsing stay on your device. We use RevenueCat and Apple to handle purchases and pricing — your vault contents are never part of that.

Encrypted at rest

Every vault file is AES-GCM encrypted with a 256-bit key that's generated on-device and stored in the iOS Keychain.

Purchases via RevenueCat & Apple

We use RevenueCat and Apple to process purchases and test pricing, so limited purchase and device data is shared with them. No ad SDKs, and your vault contents are never shared.

Permissions you control

Photos access is used only to import or export the photos you pick. Camera is used only when you take a vault photo. Face ID is optional.

You can erase everything

Wipe all vault data — every encrypted file and stored credential — directly from Settings. Uninstalling removes everything iOS allows.

Good questions

The things people ask first.

What happens if someone enters the wrong code?
Nothing alarming. A wrong gesture or PIN silently opens a believable decoy gallery built from your own photo library — no error, no warning, no hint that a real vault exists. The same wrong code always shows the same photos, so it looks completely ordinary.
Is my data uploaded anywhere?
Your vault is never uploaded — encrypted files and keys stay on your device, with no account. The only data that leaves the app is for purchases: to process payments and test pricing, limited purchase and device data is shared with Apple and RevenueCat. Your photos, contacts and browsing are never part of that.
What if I forget my pattern?
Because there's no account and your vault never leaves your device, there's no password-reset email or recovery key — that's the price of true deniability. Your vault also lives only on this phone: if you reset, replace, or remove the passcode from your device, the vault can't be recovered. Keep a backup of anything you can't lose.
How does pricing work?
Every plan starts free — your first 10 photos, no card required. To go unlimited you choose what fits you: a weekly plan ($6.99/wk), a monthly plan ($4.99/mo), a $29.99/year plan with a 3-day free trial, or a one-time $49.99 "Forever Unlock" with no subscription at all. Subscriptions renew automatically until you cancel, which you can do anytime in your App Store settings. Either way, photos you've already added are never re-locked behind a paywall.
What is App Locker?
A premium tool inside the vault that blocks other apps on your phone using Apple's Screen Time. Lock them with a single tap, automatically during a time window on the days you choose, or whenever you arrive at or leave a place. Locked apps stay on your Home Screen and show a system lock screen — they aren't hidden or deleted — and your selection never leaves your device.
Does the calculator actually work?
Yes — it's a genuinely usable calculator (and the gallery and reminders disguises are real too). That's the whole point: a convincing cover that draws no suspicion.
