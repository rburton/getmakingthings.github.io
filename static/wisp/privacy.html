Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 June 2026

The short version: Your vault — photos, videos, contacts, passwords and browsing — stays encrypted on your device and is never uploaded. To process purchases and test pricing, we use RevenueCat and Apple, which means limited purchase and device data is shared with them. We have no user accounts, run no ad SDKs, and never share your vault contents.

1. Who we are

Wisp ("the app", "we", "us") is an iOS application published by Making Things. The app is a disguised, on-device vault for storing private photos, videos, contacts, credentials and a private browser. This policy explains exactly what data the app handles and what it never touches.

2. Data we collect

We keep data collection to the minimum needed to sell and price the app. Your vault contents are never collected or uploaded.

To process purchases and run pricing experiments (for example, testing different subscription prices), we use RevenueCat alongside Apple's App Store. When you view our plans, purchase, or restore, the following is shared with RevenueCat and Apple:

  • your purchase and subscription history for this app;
  • a RevenueCat-assigned app user identifier and basic device/app information (e.g. device model, OS version, app version, country);
  • product-interaction events related to the paywall and purchases.

This data is used for app functionality (unlocking and restoring your purchase) and analytics (measuring which prices convert). RevenueCat processes it as our service provider under RevenueCat's Privacy Policy. We do not sell your data, run our own ad SDKs, or use cross-app tracking (no IDFA) — and we never collect or upload your vault contents.

3. What stays on your device

Everything you put into the vault lives only on your device:

  • Photos and videos you import are encrypted at rest using AES-GCM (256-bit) before they are written to storage. Decrypted copies exist only in memory while you are viewing, sharing, or exporting an item, and are cleared when you leave.
  • Contacts, passwords, payment cards, Wi-Fi keys, and private-browser history/bookmarks are stored inside the encrypted vault using the same on-device encryption.
  • Encryption keys, your PIN/pattern verifier, and vault settings are stored in the iOS Keychain, protected by your device passcode and biometrics where available. By default these never sync off the device.

4. Device permissions

The app requests system permissions only to deliver features you choose to use, and explains each request in plain language:

  • Photos: used only to (a) import the photos and videos you explicitly pick, (b) save items you explicitly export, and (c) display the gallery cover or Guest Vault photos you choose.
  • Camera: used when you take a new photo for the vault, and — only if you turn on Break-in Detection in Settings — to record a short, silent capture of anyone who enters the wrong pattern.
  • Face ID / Touch ID: used only as an optional, faster alternative to typing your PIN. Biometric data never leaves Apple's Secure Enclave and is never seen by the app.
  • Screen Time (Family Controls): requested only if you use App Locker, so the app can shield the other apps you choose to lock. Your selection of apps is handled privately on-device by Apple's Family Controls framework and is never uploaded to us.
  • Location: requested only if you create a location-based App Locker rule, so the app can lock or unlock the apps you chose when you arrive at or leave a place you set. Locations are evaluated on your device and never sent to us. If you never add a location rule, the app never asks for location.

You can change or revoke any of these permissions at any time in the iOS Settings app.

5. Break-in detection

If you enable Break-in Detection, the app may capture a short, silent photo or video when an incorrect pattern is entered. These captures are encrypted, stored only on your device, and never uploaded. You can view and delete them inside the vault, and disable the feature at any time. You are responsible for ensuring your use of this feature complies with the laws in your jurisdiction regarding recording other people.

6. App Locker

App Locker (a premium feature) lets you block other apps on your phone using Apple's Family Controls / Screen Time. You choose which apps to lock and when — with a single tap, on a schedule, or based on your location. The apps you select, your schedules, and any locations you set are stored only on your device and are never uploaded to us. Locked apps stay on your Home Screen and show the system lock screen; they are not hidden or deleted. You can delete any lock rule, or revoke the Screen Time and Location permissions, at any time in the app or in iOS Settings.

7. In-app purchases

Purchases and subscriptions (Weekly, Monthly, Annual, or the one-time Forever Unlock) are processed by Apple through the App Store and managed via RevenueCat. We never see or store your payment details. Apple handles limited purchase data under Apple's Privacy Policy, and RevenueCat under RevenueCat's Privacy Policy. The app reads your entitlement from RevenueCat/StoreKit and stores only a single yes/no "premium active" flag locally — never any payment data.

8. Children's privacy

Wisp is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your region).

9. What our model does — and does not — protect against

Wisp is designed to protect your privacy against someone who picks up or borrows your unlocked phone: it looks like an everyday utility, optional Guest Vaults let you keep separate spaces with separate codes, and your real content is encrypted with a key that stays in the device Keychain.

It is not designed to defend against a determined forensic adversary with a full device image, a jailbreak, or specialist extraction tools. The existence and approximate size of a vault may be visible to anyone who can inspect the app's on-device storage or an unencrypted device backup — only the contents are sealed by encryption. Please keep your expectations aligned with this model.

10. Data loss and recovery

Important: Because everything is local and keys never leave your device, there is no recovery. If you forget your pattern, reset or replace your device, restore a backup to different hardware, or remove your device passcode, your vault may become permanently inaccessible. Keep a separate backup of anything you cannot afford to lose.

11. Deleting your data

You are always in control. From Settings you can erase all vault data — every encrypted file and every stored credential — from the device. Uninstalling the app removes everything iOS permits the system to remove. Since we hold no copy of your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles data, we will update this page and the in-app policy, and revise the "last updated" date above. Material changes to data handling will be reflected before a new version that introduces them is released.
