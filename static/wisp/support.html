Support

Need help with Wisp?

We're a small team and read every message.

The fastest way to reach us: email support+wisp@getmakingthings.com. We typically reply within 2–3 business days. Because Wisp is local-only and has no account, please include your iOS version and the app version (Settings → About) so we can help quickly — never include your pattern, PIN, or any vault contents.

System requirements

Wisp runs on iPhone with iOS 15.6 or later. It is an iPhone app; iPad and Mac are not supported. Everything happens on-device — there is no account to create and no sign-in.

Common questions

What is a Guest Vault?
A Guest Vault is an optional second vault with its own code. You fill it yourself with photos you pick or built-in sample images, so what you share and what you keep private stay separate. Wisp never scans your library or builds a guest space automatically.
I forgot my pattern. How do I recover my vault?
Because Wisp has no account and your keys never leave your device, there is no password reset and no recovery — that's the price of true privacy. If you forget your pattern, reset or replace your device, or remove your device passcode, your vault may become permanently inaccessible. Always keep a separate backup of anything you can't afford to lose.
How do I open the real vault?
From the disguise screen, use the hidden gesture you set up during onboarding, then enter your pattern or PIN. If you've forgotten how you set it up, reinstalling will not recover an existing vault — the unlock gesture and credential you chose are the only way in.
Is my data uploaded or backed up anywhere?
No. Wisp is local-only. Your photos, videos, contacts, passwords and browsing are encrypted on your device and never leave it. The app only contacts Apple's App Store (via RevenueCat) to process purchases. See our Privacy Policy for the full detail.
Why does Wisp ask for Photos, Camera, Screen Time or Location access?
Photos access is used only to import the items you pick, save items you export, display the gallery cover, and add photos you choose to a Guest Vault. Camera access is used when you take a new photo for the vault, and — only if you enable Break-in Detection — to capture anyone entering the wrong pattern. Screen Time access is requested only if you use App Locker, so it can shield the apps you choose to lock; Location access is requested only if you set a location-based lock rule, and is evaluated on your device. You can change or revoke any of these permissions anytime in iOS Settings.
I'm stuck on the free limit. How many photos are free?
The free tier stores your first 10 photos or videos in one hidden vault, with no card required. To add more — and to unlock Contacts, the Credentials manager, App Locker, and Intruder Detection — upgrade to any paid plan. Photos you've already added are never re-locked behind the paywall.

Manage your subscription

All billing is handled by Apple through the App Store — we never see or store your payment details. To view, change, or cancel a Wisp subscription:

  • Open the Settings app on your iPhone.
  • Tap your name at the top, then Subscriptions.
  • Select Wisp and choose a different plan or Cancel Subscription.

Cancelling stops future renewals; you keep access until the end of the current billing period. For Apple's step-by-step guide, see How to cancel a subscription from Apple. The one-time "Forever Unlock" is not a subscription and never renews.

Restore purchases & new devices

If you've upgraded and your purchase isn't showing — or you've moved to a new iPhone — open Wisp's Settings and tap Restore Purchases while signed in to the same Apple ID you bought with. Note that your purchase restores, but your vault contents do not transfer between devices, because they are encrypted locally and never uploaded.

Refunds

Refunds for App Store purchases are handled by Apple. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com using the Apple ID that made the purchase. If you believe you were charged in error, email us and we'll do what we can to help.

Data & privacy

Everything you put into Wisp stays encrypted on your device. We have no user accounts, run no ad SDKs for paying users, and never collect or upload your vault contents. For the complete picture, read our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
