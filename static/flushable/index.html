The sit

Five minutes. One thumb. Oldest first.

Swipe left to flush, right to keep, down for later. A sit clock ticks in the corner, and when it hits zero we flush you off the bowl, with a recap of the space you freed.

Smart, on your phone

It spots the blurry, the duplicated and the 2016 screenshots.

Flushable uses Apple’s on-device Vision to flag soft focus, near-duplicates and screenshots, and picks the sharpest shot in a burst. Your photos never leave your device. It even works in airplane mode.

Questions

Does Flushable upload my photos?

No. Everything happens on your iPhone or iPad. Flushable has no servers and no account, and it collects no data at all. See the privacy policy.

How much space will it free?

It depends on your library. Old videos, screen recordings and bursts free the most. Flushable shows the size of every photo and the total waiting in the Bowl, so you know before you clean up.

What happens after the 7 days?

Your free week ends and new sits pause. Unlock Flushable for $9.99 once to keep going. You can still browse your piles and empty the Bowl either way.

Will I be charged automatically?

No. The free week isn’t a subscription and nothing renews. You’re only charged if you choose to unlock.

How do I get a deleted photo back?

Open Photos › Albums › Recently Deleted. Photos keeps emptied items there for 30 days. Before you empty the Bowl, you can rescue any photo right in Flushable.

Why does it want full access to my library?

To move photos you’ve chosen to Recently Deleted, Photos requires write access. Flushable never deletes on its own, only when you tap Empty the Bowl and confirm.
