System requirements

📱
iPhone running iOS 18.0 or later. Handshot is iPhone-only. iPad and Mac are not supported.

Common questions

🖐

Handshot isn't detecting my hand

Good lighting is everything. Stand 0.5 to 1.5 meters from the camera with your palm facing the lens. A plain background helps. Gloves and very low light break detection.

⏱️

My gesture isn't triggering the photo

Hold the pose steady for about half a second. Handshot waits for three stable frames before it fires — that's the safeguard against accidental shots. If it still won't trigger, try stepping closer.

📸

Camera preview is black

Camera permission is off. Open iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Camera and turn Handshot on. Then relaunch the app.

💾

My photo didn't save to Photos

Handshot needs add-only permission for your photo library. Open iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos → Handshot and allow adding photos.

🏷️

My photos have a "Shot with Handshot" watermark

The watermark is on Free-tier captures. Upgrade to Handshot Pro and it's removed from every new photo. Existing watermarked photos stay as they are.

🎬

I can't record video

Video recording is a Pro feature. Tap the Pro upsell in the app or start a free trial to unlock it along with burst mode, pinch-to-zoom, and all countdown lengths.

🔍

Pinch-to-zoom isn't working

Gesture zoom (pinch and spread) is a Pro feature. On Free you still get full photo capture with open-palm, just without zoom control.

⚡

Countdown feels too fast or too slow

Open Handshot, tap Settings, and set your default countdown from 0 (instant) up to 5 seconds. Free tier supports 0 and 3 seconds. Pro unlocks 1, 2, 4, and 5.

Manage your subscription

Every Handshot subscription and the Lifetime purchase are billed by Apple through the App Store — we never see your payment details. That also means cancellation and renewal live in your Apple ID settings, not inside Handshot.

To cancel, switch plans, or see your next renewal date:

  1. Open Settings on your iPhone
  2. Tap your name at the top
  3. Tap Subscriptions
  4. Tap Handshot and make your changes

Apple's official guide: How to cancel a subscription from Apple.

Restore purchases on a new device

Signed in with the same Apple ID? Open Handshot, go to Settings → Restore Purchases. Your Pro status and any active subscription are tied to your Apple ID and will come back automatically.

Refunds

Apple handles every App Store refund directly — by design, we have no access to the payment side of your account and can't issue a refund ourselves. Apple is usually responsive and fair about it.

Request a refund here: reportaproblem.apple.com. Sign in with your Apple ID, find the Handshot purchase, tap Report a Problem, and pick the reason that fits.

Your photos and data

Every frame Handshot sees is processed on your iPhone. No camera data, photos, videos, or hand pose coordinates ever leave your device. We don't run a cloud — there's literally no server on our side that holds your stuff.
