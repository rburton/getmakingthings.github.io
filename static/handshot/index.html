Why Handshot

Built Different. On Purpose.

Every feature exists because timers suck and Bluetooth remotes die at the worst moment.

👍

Thumbs Up = Switch Modes

Toggle between photo and video with a thumbs up. See the mode change instantly on screen. No need to touch your phone — switch mid-session from across the room.

🖐

Open Palm = Capture

One gesture does it all. In photo mode, open palm takes a picture. In video mode, open palm starts recording. Raise it again or thumbs down to stop. Simple.

👤

Smart Owner Detection

In group settings, Handshot learns who's holding the phone and only responds to that person's gestures. Friends can wave, point, or high-five without accidentally triggering a shot.

🎬

Hands-Free Video + Auto-Trim

Start recording with your palm. Handshot automatically cuts the part where your hand is still up, so your clips start clean. Perfect for gym sets and tutorials. Pro feature.

📳

You'll Feel the Countdown

Feel a tap for each second. Hear "3… 2… 1… Say cheese!" You'll know exactly when to smile — even from across the gym or across the room.

📸

Burst Mode

Capture up to 6 rapid-fire photos per gesture. Pick the best frame later. Never miss the perfect expression. Pro feature.

🔍

Gesture Zoom Control

Pinch to zoom in, spread to zoom out. Frame your shot perfectly from across the room without touching the phone. Pro feature.

🧠

Sees Your Hand Instantly

On-device AI recognizes your hand the moment you raise it. No delay, no cloud processing, no internet required. Works offline.

🔦

See the Flash Count Down

In dim rooms, your phone's light pulses with the countdown so you can see the beat from across the room.

🔄

Front & Back Camera

Switch between selfie and rear camera. Use the front camera for form checks or the back camera for group photos and scenery.

🖼️

Gallery with Swipe Review

Review your captures instantly. Swipe right to keep, left to delete. Undo if you change your mind. Share via AirDrop, Messages, and more.

🚫

No Ads. Ever.

Handshot will never show you ads. We make money from Pro subscriptions, not by selling your attention. Clean, focused, respectful.

🛡️

Your Privacy is Non-Negotiable

Everything happens on your iPhone. Your photos, your camera feed, your hand movements — none of it ever leaves your device. We don't have servers. We don't collect data. We don't even know you exist — and we like it that way.
