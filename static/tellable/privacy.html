Privacy Policy

Effective 9 October 2026

Tellable collects no data. Nothing you read, scan or import is sent to us, and there is no account to create.

What Tellable does not do

  • No accounts. You never sign in.
  • No analytics and no crash reporting services.
  • No tracking, no advertising identifier, no ads.
  • No third-party software kits that send data anywhere. The open-source parts of the app, including the voices and the speech engines, run on your device.
  • Nothing is sent to Tellable. We have no server that receives data about you.

The App Store privacy label for Tellable reads "Data Not Collected".

Where your documents live

Documents you scan, import or paste, your reading position, your settings, and the audio Tellable renders so it can play without delay are all stored inside the app on your device. They are protected by the device's file encryption. Deleting a document in Tellable removes it, and deleting the app removes everything.

When Tellable uses the network

Tellable works offline. Its voices are built in, so nothing is downloaded to read aloud. The app itself uses the network in one case, which you choose.

Reading a web link

If you paste a link, Tellable fetches that page once, on your request, with the pictures in it, so it can read it to you. While you wait, Tellable also loads the page's style sheets, fonts and pictures to show you a preview of the page as it looks in a browser. No script on the page runs, no cookies are kept or sent, nothing else on the page is loaded, and the preview is deleted as soon as the article is ready. To find the article itself, Tellable runs Mozilla's Readability (the reader view of Firefox) on your device, inside that same sealed view; it ships with the app and sends nothing anywhere. Only the article's own pictures are fetched. Tellable does not fetch the page again unless you ask. A link you share from Safari brings the page as Safari shows it, and Tellable makes no request for it at all; a link shared from another app, or one that waits for a free spot, is fetched once and read the same way, with no preview and nothing loaded but the page.

Apple's services

Purchases go through Apple's App Store, which uses the network on Apple's side (see Purchases below). The renewal reminder for the yearly plan is a notification scheduled on your device; no server sends it.

Purchases

Tellable Pro is sold through the App Store. Apple handles payment, billing, renewal and refunds, and Tellable only checks on the device whether a purchase is active. We do not see your name, your payment details or your Apple Account.

Children

Tellable collects no data from anyone, including children. The App Store age rating describes the app's content.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new effective date. The app's behaviour described here is also checked by automated tests in the project.

Contact

Questions about privacy go to support+tellable@getmakingthings.com.
