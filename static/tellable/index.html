Clean Read skips page numbers, headers and footers, footnote marks and citations. What it skipped stays on the page, dimmed, and a tap lets you hear it.

Listen your way

Choose a speed from 0.5x to 3x. Play and pause from the Lock Screen, and set a sleep timer for bed. Bookmark a sentence or write a note on it, then find it again in a tap.

With Pro, line up chapters and articles in Up Next, save a document as audio to take anywhere, and play your notes back or share them as audio.

Private by design

Tellable collects no data, and there is no account. It goes online only to fetch a link you paste.

Want your library on your iPad too? Turn on iCloud sync. It is off until you choose it, and it uses your own private iCloud, which we cannot see.
