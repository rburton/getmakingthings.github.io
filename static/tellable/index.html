Made with ADHD in mind

Lost your place again?
Tellable kept it.

Reading a page and staying on it are two different jobs. Tellable does the second one for you: the voice carries you forward, the highlight shows exactly where you are, and when your mind drifts a glance brings you back.

Two ways in

Hear it and see it at once.

The word you hear is the word that is lit. Ears and eyes on the same word give a wandering mind one anchor instead of a wall of text.

Drift happens

Find your place in a glance.

The sentence is tinted and the spoken word is boxed, so looking back finds you in a second. Missed a bit? Tap a word and it reads again from that sentence.

Eyes still

One word at a time.

The full player shows each word large and centred, in time with the voice, so there is no line to lose and no page to wander off.

Less noise

The clutter goes quiet.

Clean Read skips page numbers, headers, footnote marks and citations, so nothing breaks the flow of the sentence you are on.

Your pace

From 0.5x to 3x.

Some people find a brisker pace holds their attention better. Speed up until the page holds you, or slow down for the hard parts.

Pick it up later

Stop any time, start right there.

Walk away mid-sentence and Tellable opens on that sentence next time. Bookmark what matters and set a sleep timer for the end of the chapter.

Tellable is a reading tool, not a treatment. It was designed with ADHD and dyslexia in mind, and it works for anyone whose attention likes to wander.

What it does

From paper to voice in a few seconds.

Scan

Frame the page. Watch it read.

The camera draws a frame for one page or an open book and takes the shot when the page is lined up and sharp. Then each line lights up as it is recognized, so you can see nothing was missed. Columns, two-page spreads and photos from your library all work.

Clean Read

Hear the words, not the clutter.

Page numbers, headers, footnote marks and citations are skipped out loud, but stay on the page, dimmed. Tap one to hear it, or jump to its footnote.

Tables

Tables that make sense out loud.

Rows are read with their headers, so every number comes with its name. Long tables can be skipped, and a tap reads one when you want it.

Your page

Fonts and pages that ease reading.

Atkinson Hyperlegible or OpenDyslexic, on a white, cream or dark page, at the text size that suits you, up to the largest. The highlight keeps up whatever you choose.

Words as they are spoken

One word at a time, in time with the voice.

The full player shows each word large and centred, its focus letter in coral, so your eyes never have to travel. Turn the phone sideways and the words fill the screen.

Ask

Tellable writes study notes or a deck of flashcards from the document, to the focus you type. Flip a card, jump to the page it came from, or let Listen read question, pause, answer for you. With Apple Intelligence on iOS 26, with Pro.

Questions

Is there a monthly subscription?

No. Tellable Pro is one payment that is yours for good. A yearly plan is there for people who prefer one; it renews once a year until you cancel in Settings, and Tellable can remind you three days before it does.

Is there a free trial that turns into a charge?

No. Instead of a trial, five documents are free for as long as you like, with every voice, the highlight and Clean Read. You only pay if you decide you want more, and nothing is charged unless you choose a plan.

Why does it cost so much less than other read-aloud apps?

The voices run on your iPhone or iPad, not on our servers, so there is no server bill to pass on to you every month. That is why a single payment can cover it.

Does Tellable help with ADHD?

Many people with ADHD find it easier to stay with a page when they hear it and see each word lit at the same time, and to find their place again after their mind wanders. Tellable is a reading tool, not a treatment, and it never asks about a diagnosis.

Does it really work with no internet?

Yes. The voices, the scanning and the reading all run on your iPhone or iPad. Only fetching a web link needs a connection.

Will it read my textbook?

If you can open it on your phone as a PDF, EPUB, Word file or DRM-free Kindle file, or point the camera at it, Tellable can read it. Books bought with copy protection stay locked: Tellable never removes it.

Can it read lecture slides and scanned handouts?

Yes. Slides saved as a PDF read slide by slide, and scanned pages are read on your device, including two-column layouts.

Do Ask, summaries and study guides send my documents anywhere?

No. They use Apple Intelligence on your device. They need iOS 26 on an iPhone or iPad that supports Apple Intelligence, and Tellable Pro.

Can I use it on my iPhone and my iPad?

Yes. Tellable is one app for both, with a layout made for each. Turn on iCloud sync and your library and reading place follow you.
