How Wobbly teaches

The same four steps, every letter, every time, so a child always knows what happens next. It is the whole method, and there is nothing else to learn.

  1. 1 👀

    Watch

    Wobbly draws every stroke first. A traveling dot and a spoken prompt show exactly where to start and which way to go, and a little gloved hand rides the line as it is drawn, so a child who cannot read a word yet still knows exactly what is being asked.

  2. 2 ✏️

    Trace

    Friendly arrows and gentle vibrations guide little fingers, and the tracing is deliberately forgiving, so trying always feels good. Start a stroke from the wrong end and Wobbly quietly rewinds and points at the right place, because a backwards letter looks fine while rehearsing a habit that is expensive to undo later. The pairs children flip most, b and d, p and q, get their own gentle lesson.

  3. 3 🔊

    Hear

    Every letter has its sound and a picture word (“A is for Apple”), so writing and reading reinforce each other from the very first day.

  4. 4 ⭐

    Celebrate

    Finishing always earns at least one star, plus collectible stickers for first tries, comebacks, and personal bests. Coaching is specific and kind, spoken aloud: “Let’s make the tall line again!” Because most four-year-olds can’t read yet.

An adventure, not a worksheet

📚 Interactive storybooks

Thirteen storybooks where every word is one your child can already sound out. Poke the pictures and they squeak, pop, and moo; finished books wait on your child’s very own bookshelf to read again.

🧭 Hands-free reading mode

Autopilot carries your child from word to word and stop to stop, no menus to find. Put the app down mid-lesson and a bookmark reopens the exact same step next time.

🎤 Say it back

Tap “Try it” and Wobbly listens while your child says the sound, word, or sentence, then praises what was right. All listening happens on your device; the audio is never stored and never sent anywhere.

Praise the effort, grow the child

The way we talk to children while they learn shapes how hard they try, and whether they want to try again at all. Wobbly is built around one well-studied idea from early-childhood research: praise the effort and the progress, never the “smart.” Kids who learn that ability grows with practice take on harder things, bounce back from stumbles, and go further. Every cheer, star, and gentle nudge is designed to build exactly that.

“You worked so hard on that tall line!” teaches a child far more than “You’re so clever.” One grows with every try. The other quietly whispers that a stumble means you’re not.

Wobbly only ever says the first kind.
  • 🌱

    A growth mindset from stroke one

    Wobbly praises what your child did, not who they are: the effort, the persistence, the progress. That is the mindset researchers tie most closely to children who keep going when things get tricky.

  • 🛟

    Safe to be wrong

    No buzzers, no red marks, no failure screens. Off-path ink is a soft blue, never a scold. When mistakes stop feeling scary, children take the small, brave risks that real learning is made of.

  • 🗣️

    Specific, spoken encouragement

    Instead of an empty “good job,” Wobbly names the win out loud: “Let’s make the round part again!” Concrete, kind coaching helps pre-readers know just what they did well and what to try next.

  • ❤️

    Confidence that lasts

    No timers, no pressure, no punishments. Finishing always earns a star, so your child walks away from every letter thinking “I can do this,” and wanting to come back tomorrow.
