Find shows no places

Perch covers 27 cities, not everywhere. When there is nothing within 3 miles, Find says so and offers the list of cities Perch covers. You can also open it any time: tap Locations on Find, or open Settings and tap Locations.

A place's hours or amenities are wrong

Hours and amenities change without notice, so call ahead before a trip that matters. To report a correction, open Settings and tap Send a catalog correction, or email us with the place's name and what is wrong.

I don't want to share my location

You don't have to. Type a ZIP code into the search field on Find, and Perch sorts places by distance from that ZIP code instead. To change the permission later, open iPhone or iPad Settings, then Perch, then Location.

Does Perch work offline?

Yes. Once the catalog has loaded, the list, filters, place pages and saved places all work without a connection. Travel times need a connection, because Apple calculates them.

A saved place says "No longer listed"

That place has left the catalog. It stays in Saved, with the name and address it had, so you don't lose it. Remove it from Saved when you no longer need it.

Do my saved places sync between devices?

No. Saved places stay on the device where you saved them. Perch has no accounts and no servers that could hold them.

Accessibility

Perch supports VoiceOver, Dynamic Type and Reduce Motion, and it states open or closed in words rather than by color. If something doesn't work with your assistive technology, email us; those reports come first.

For App Review

  • No account or sign-in is needed, and there is no demo account.
  • Location is optional. If it is declined, type ZIP code 11211 (Brooklyn, New York) on Find to see places.
  • There are no purchases, subscriptions or advertising.
  • The catalog ships inside the app, so every screen works without a connection.
