Privacy Policy

For Perch on iPhone and iPad. Effective 13 September 2026. Making Things LLC.

The short version. Perch has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Places you save stay on your device. Your location is used on the device to sort places by distance, and it is never sent to Perch.

1. Who this covers

This policy covers the Perch app for iPhone and iPad, published by Making Things LLC ("we", "us"), and this website.

2. No account

Perch has no sign-up, no login and no user profile. There is nothing to sign in to and nothing to sign up for. We do not know who you are, and we have no way to link the app to you.

3. What the app stores, and where

Perch stores the following on your device only:

  • Saved places. The place's catalog identifier, when you saved it, and its last-known name and address, so a saved place can still be shown if it leaves the catalog.
  • Settings. Your default amenity filters, and whether you have seen the welcome screen.
  • A copy of the catalog. The list of places, so Perch works without a connection.

Saved places are not synced, not backed up to Perch, and not sent anywhere.

4. What leaves the device, and why

RequestSent toWhat it contains
Catalog update Perch's catalog host The name of a city file, and nothing else: no identifier, no device information and no location.
Travel time on a place's page Apple Your location and the place's location, so Apple can estimate a walking or driving route. It happens only while that page is open, and only when your location is available. Perch does not store the answer.
ZIP code search Apple The ZIP code you typed, so Apple can return its location.
Directions, calls, email and websites Apple's apps Perch hands the address, number or link to Maps, Phone, Mail or Safari. Perch does not proxy or record them.
Sharing a place The system share sheet Plain text about the place. Perch does not learn what you chose or who you sent it to.

Apple's handling of these requests is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

5. Location

Perch asks for location as While Using the App, never Always. It is used on the device to sort places by how near they are, and for the travel estimate described above. It is never sent to the catalog host. If you decline, search by ZIP code instead; everything else keeps working. You can change the permission at any time in iPhone or iPad Settings.

6. What we do not do

  • No analytics, attribution or crash reporting software.
  • No advertising and no advertising identifier.
  • No tracking across apps or websites.
  • No selling or sharing of personal information, to anyone.

In Apple's App Privacy terms, Perch is Data Not Collected.

7. When you contact us

If you email support+perch@getmakingthings.com, we receive your email address and what you write. We use it to answer you and keep the thread only as long as it is useful. We do not add you to any list.

8. Children

Perch is not directed at children. It has no accounts, no messaging and no user-generated content, and it collects nothing from anyone, of any age.

9. Your control over your data

  • Remove a saved place from the Saved tab.
  • Withdraw location in iPhone or iPad Settings, at any time.
  • Delete the app to remove everything it stored.

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to hand over or delete on request.

10. This website

This site is static. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics and embeds no trackers. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses and requested pages, for security and reliability. We do not use them to identify anyone.

11. Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date at the top changes with it, and a material change will be noted in the app's release notes before it ships.
