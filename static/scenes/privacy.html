Privacy Policy

For Scenes on iPhone. Effective 10 September 2026. Making Things LLC.

The short version. Scenes has no accounts and no servers. It does not collect, sell or share your data, and it contains no analytics, advertising or tracking software of any kind. What you put into the app stays on your iPhone, and if you turn on sync it stays in your own iCloud account, which we cannot read.

1. Who this covers

This policy covers the Scenes iPhone app, its widgets, its Control Center controls and its App Intents, all published by Making Things LLC ("we", "us"). It also covers this website.

2. No account, no profile

Scenes has no sign-up, no login, no email capture and no user profile. We do not know who you are, and we have no way to link the app to you. Your App Store purchase is handled by Apple under your Apple Account, and we never see your Apple Account, your name, your email address or your payment details.

3. What the app stores, and where

Everything you create in Scenes is stored on your device, in an app group container shared by the app and its widgets:

  • Your scenes, their names, types, colors and checklist items.
  • Your sessions: when a scene was live and when it stopped, which is what the record is built from.
  • Your gym set logs, errand stops and any address you type into a stop.
  • Your reminders and their schedules.
  • App settings, such as haptics, the chosen app icon and whether sync is on.
  • A small snapshot file that lets widgets, controls and the Live Activity draw the current scene without launching the app.

None of it is sent to us. There is no Scenes server to send it to.

4. iCloud sync

If you turn on sync, the data above is stored in your private CloudKit database, inside your own iCloud account, so your devices can agree with each other. Apple encrypts it and we cannot read it, list it, or recover it for you. Its handling is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Turn sync off in Settings and the app goes back to storing data on the device only. To remove the synced copy, sign out of iCloud, disable Scenes in iCloud settings, or delete the app's iCloud data from iPhone Settings.

5. Permissions, and what each one is for

Scenes asks for a permission only at the moment the feature that needs it is used, and it stays useful if you say no.

PermissionAsked whenWhat it is used for
Notifications After your first Deep Work or Gym start To tell you a focus block or rest timer ended. Notifications are composed and scheduled on your device. There are no marketing notifications and no push server.
Location, While Using On your first errand stop that has an address To show how far the next stop is. The distance is calculated on your device and is not stored, logged or transmitted. Scenes never asks for "Always" and never tracks you in the background. Decline it and the checklist still works; only the distance disappears.
Health Only if you open a walk, run, meditation or gym scene and choose to connect it Reading: workouts, workout effort, mindful minutes, and, if you allow it, heart rate, steps, distance and active energy for the minutes your session ran. Writing: the workout itself, an effort score, mindful minutes, and a State of Mind entry if you log a mood.
Alarms The first time you arm a reminder or a timed alert To ring at the end of a block, a rest set or a reminder, even on silent. Alarms are scheduled locally on your device.

Health data in particular

Health data is read for display only. Nothing Scenes reads from Health is stored by the app, included in iCloud sync, or included in your export. It is fetched when a screen needs it and discarded when the screen is done with it. It is never used for advertising, never shared with anyone, and never leaves your device. You can withdraw access at any time in iPhone Settings under Privacy and Security, then Health.

6. What we do not do

  • No third-party analytics, attribution, or crash reporting SDKs.
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No tracking across apps or websites, so Scenes never shows the App Tracking Transparency prompt.
  • No selling or sharing of personal information, under any definition, to anyone.
  • No data brokers, no mailing list, no profiling.

In Apple's App Privacy terms, Scenes is Data Not Collected.

7. Purchases

Subscriptions are sold by Apple through the App Store. Apple tells the app whether a subscription is currently active, and nothing else. We receive only Apple's aggregate sales and subscription reports, which contain no personal information. We never receive your card details, your billing address or your Apple Account identifier.

8. When you contact us

If you email support+scenes@getmakingthings.com, we receive your email address and whatever you write, including any screenshot or export you choose to attach. We use it to answer you, and we keep the thread only as long as it is useful for support. We do not add you to any list. Please do not send us health information; we do not need it to help you.

9. Children

Scenes is rated 4+ and is safe for all ages, but it is not directed at children, it has no accounts, no messaging and no user-generated public content, and it knowingly collects nothing from anyone, of any age.

10. Your control over your data

  • Export. Settings, then Export data, writes a JSON file of your scenes and record that you can share anywhere.
  • Delete. Deleting the app removes the local data with it.
  • Withdraw a permission. Notifications, Location and Health can each be revoked in iPhone Settings, at any time, without breaking the app.
  • Stop syncing. Turn sync off in Settings, and remove the iCloud copy from iPhone Settings.

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to hand over or delete on request. If you are in a region with data protection rights such as the GDPR or the CCPA, those rights are satisfied by the fact that we are not the holder of your data: you are.

11. This website

This site is static. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics and embeds no trackers. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses and requested pages, for security and reliability. We do not use those logs to identify you.

12. Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date at the top changes with it, and a material change will be noted in the app's release notes. Continuing to use Scenes after a change means the updated policy applies.
