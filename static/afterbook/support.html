What happens when the free week ends?

If you did nothing, your plan starts and Apple charges your Apple Account. If you cancelled, you keep full access until the trial's end date. Then Afterbook locks until you subscribe. Your progress is kept, and it comes back when you subscribe or restore. You get one free trial per Apple Account.

How do I cancel?

Open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Afterbook, and tap Cancel Subscription. You can also tap Manage subscription in the You tab. Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial or period ends to avoid the next charge. You keep access until the period ends.

How do I restore my purchase?

On a new device, or after reinstalling, sign in with the same Apple Account and tap Restore Purchases. It is on the paywall and in the You tab. Restoring needs an internet connection.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

I found a mistake on a card

Thank you. Open the You tab and tap Report a card mistake. It tells us which card you were on, so we can find it fast. You can also email us with the opening name and what looks wrong. Fixes ship in an app update.

Does Afterbook work offline?

Yes. Lessons, drills, the structure library, and your repertoire all work without a connection. Buying a subscription and restoring a purchase need a connection, because Apple handles them. Progress syncs through iCloud the next time you are online.

Do I need an account?

No. Afterbook has no sign-up. Your subscription uses your Apple Account, and your progress syncs between your devices through your own iCloud.

My progress is not on my other device

Check that both devices use the same Apple Account and that iCloud is on for Afterbook in the Settings app. Then open Afterbook on both while online.
