Privacy Policy

Effective September 13, 2026. Afterbook for iPhone and iPad, by Making Things LLC ("we", "us").

The short version:

  • No account. You never give us your name or email to use Afterbook.
  • No tracking, no advertising, and no advertising identifier.
  • We do not sell or share your data.
  • Your progress stays on your devices and in your own iCloud.

1. What Afterbook stores

Afterbook stores your learning progress: the openings you chose, lessons you finished, drill results, review dates, and your settings. This lives on your device. We do not run servers that hold it, and we cannot see it.

2. iCloud sync

If iCloud is on for your Apple Account, Afterbook syncs your progress between your devices with iCloud key-value storage. Apple stores and processes that data under Apple's Privacy Policy. It is tied to your Apple Account, not to us. You can turn off iCloud for Afterbook in the Settings app.

3. Purchases

Subscriptions and free trials are handled by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. Afterbook checks on your device, through Apple, whether you have an active trial or subscription. Apple may share limited, non-identifying sales reports with us (for example, how many trials started).

4. No tracking and no advertising

  • Afterbook has no ads and no advertising SDK.
  • Afterbook does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA) and does not ask to track you across apps or websites.
  • Afterbook contains no third-party analytics SDK.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share personal data with anyone.

5. Crash and performance diagnostics

Afterbook uses Apple's tools to learn about crashes and slowdowns: MetricKit, TestFlight, and App Store Connect. Apple sends us these reports only if you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device settings (Settings, Privacy and Security, Analytics and Improvements). The reports describe the app and device (for example, app version, iOS version, and where a crash happened). They do not include your name, email, or progress. You can turn this off at any time in the same place.

6. Emails you send us

If you write to support, or use "Report a card mistake" in the app, we receive what you choose to send: your email address, your message, and for card reports the card's identifier and any screenshot you attach. We use it only to reply and fix the problem, and we delete it when it is no longer needed.

7. Notifications

If you ask for a trial reminder, Afterbook schedules a local notification on your device. Nothing is sent to us.

8. Children

Afterbook is rated 4+. It has no chat, no social features, and no user-generated content, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us personal information by email, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Your choices

  • Delete Afterbook to remove its data from that device. To remove synced progress, also turn off iCloud for Afterbook, or manage iCloud storage in the Settings app.
  • Manage or cancel your subscription in the Settings app, under your name, then Subscriptions.
  • Wherever you live, you can ask us any question about your data. Because we hold almost none, most requests are simple.

10. Changes to this policy

If our practices change, we will update this page and the effective date above. If a change affects what data leaves your device, we will say so in the app before it takes effect.
