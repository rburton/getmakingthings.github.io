2. Description of Service

Handshot is an iOS camera application that uses on-device hand gesture recognition to allow users to take photos and videos hands-free. The App uses Apple's Vision framework to detect hand poses through the device camera and trigger camera actions based on recognized gestures.

Key features of the App include:

  • Hand gesture recognition — The App detects hand gestures (such as open palm, fist, finger counts, thumbs up, thumbs down, and pinch gestures) to control camera functions.
  • Photo capture — Trigger photo capture using hand gestures with configurable countdown timers.
  • Video recording — Start and stop video recording using hand gestures (Pro feature).
  • Zoom control — Adjust camera zoom using pinch gestures (Pro feature).
  • On-device processing — All hand detection and image processing occurs entirely on your device. No camera data, photos, videos, or hand landmark data is transmitted off your device.

5. Free and Pro Tiers

Handshot is available in two tiers:

Free Tier

The Free tier is available to all users at no cost and includes:

  • Open palm gesture for photo capture
  • Thumbs down gesture for cancellation
  • Countdown options of 0 seconds (instant) and 3 seconds
  • Sessions limited to 10 photos
  • A subtle "Shot with Handshot" watermark on saved photos

Pro Tier

The Pro tier unlocks the full capabilities of the App, including:

  • All hand gestures (fist, peace sign, finger counts, pinch zoom, thumbs up for mode switching)
  • All countdown options (0, 1, 2, 3, 4, and 5 seconds)
  • Burst mode for rapid photo capture
  • Sessions of up to 50 photos
  • No watermark on saved photos
  • Video recording via hand gestures
  • All future Pro features as they are released

Features available only to Pro subscribers will be indicated in the App with a lock icon or similar visual indicator. Attempting to use a Pro-only gesture or feature on the Free tier will not perform the action.
