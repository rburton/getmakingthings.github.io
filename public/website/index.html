How It Works

Three steps to storytime magic

1

Scan

Open any children's book and point your camera. StoryGlow captures each page with automatic perspective correction and instantly recognizes the text.

2

Listen

AI narration reads the story aloud with warm, natural-sounding voices. Words highlight one by one in golden glow, so your child can follow along effortlessly.

Learn

Tap any word to hear it spoken. Pages turn with a satisfying curl animation. Your child builds vocabulary and reading confidence through play — no lessons required.
