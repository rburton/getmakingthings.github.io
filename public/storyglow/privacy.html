Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 1, 2026

StoryGlow ("we", "our", "the app") is committed to protecting the privacy of you and your children. This policy explains what data the app handles and how it is stored.

Our Core Principle

Your data never leaves your device. StoryGlow is designed to work entirely on-device. We do not operate servers, do not collect analytics, and do not create user accounts.

Data Stored on Your Device

The following data is created and stored locally on your iPhone or iPad:

  • Scanned page images — Photos of book pages you scan are saved in the app's private storage directory. They are not added to your Photo Library.
  • Recognized text (OCR) — Text extracted from scanned pages is stored in a local Core Data database on your device. OCR is performed entirely on-device using Apple's Vision framework.
  • Narration audio — Generated audio files are created on-device using text-to-speech and stored locally alongside the book data.
  • Word timing data — Timestamps for word-level highlighting are computed on-device and stored in the local database.
  • Parent PIN — If you set a parent PIN, it is stored securely in the iOS Keychain on your device. It is never transmitted.
  • Subscription status — Your subscription entitlement status is cached in the iOS Keychain for offline access. Subscription transactions are managed entirely by Apple.
  • App preferences — Settings such as font choice and accessibility preferences are stored in the app's local UserDefaults.

Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect personal information, names, email addresses, or account credentials.
  • We do not use analytics, crash reporting, or tracking services.
  • We do not collect or transmit the text of scanned books.
  • We do not collect device identifiers, IP addresses, or location data.
  • We do not use advertising SDKs or ad networks.
  • We do not use cookies or web tracking.

Third-Party Services

StoryGlow uses the following Apple system services:

  • Apple Vision Framework — For on-device optical character recognition (OCR). All processing happens locally on your device. No data is sent to Apple.
  • Apple StoreKit — For processing subscription purchases and verifying entitlements. Purchase transactions are handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • Apple VNDocumentCameraViewController — For the document scanning camera interface. Captured images remain on your device.
  • Local Authentication (Face ID / Touch ID) — For parent mode biometric authentication. Biometric data is handled entirely by the iOS Secure Enclave and is never accessible to StoryGlow.

No other third-party services, SDKs, or APIs are used.

Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

StoryGlow is designed for use by children under the supervision of a parent or guardian. We are committed to complying with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA):

  • We do not collect any personal information from children (or adults).
  • We do not require account creation.
  • We do not use behavioral advertising.
  • The app does not require an internet connection for core functionality.
  • Parent-only features are protected by Face ID, Touch ID, or a 4-digit PIN.

Data Deletion

You are in full control of your data:

  • Delete a book: Deleting a book from your library permanently removes all associated data — scanned images, OCR text, narration audio, and word timing data.
  • Delete the app: Uninstalling StoryGlow from your device removes all app data, including the local database, stored images, audio files, and Keychain entries.

Data Security

All data is stored within the app's sandboxed storage on your device, protected by iOS's built-in security measures. Sensitive data (parent PIN, subscription status) is stored in the iOS Keychain, which is encrypted by the device hardware.

Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes to this privacy policy, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top. Since StoryGlow does not collect email addresses or contact information, changes will be communicated through the App Store update notes.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at privacy@storyglow.app.
